About

I am a PhD candidate studying the hydrodynamics of solid and perforated heave plates for floating offshore wind turbines (FOWTs), combining CFD with PIV experiments. My longer-term aim is to become a specialist in computational fluid dynamics and fluid mechanics more broadly.
